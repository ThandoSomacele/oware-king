How to play
  1. The six pits on your side are yours. Tap one to pick up all its seeds.
  2. The seeds are sown one per pit, counter-clockwise, onto your opponent's side and back round.
  3. If your last seed makes a pit on your opponent's side hold 2 or 3, you capture it. Pits just before it that also hold 2 or 3 are captured too.
  4. If your opponent has no seeds, you must play a move that gives them some.
  5. There are 48 seeds. The first player to capture 25 wins.
