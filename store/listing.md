## Full description
Oware is the seed game played across West Africa for hundreds of years. Oware King brings it to your phone, tablet, PC and TV.

One tap sows your seeds around the board. Capture pits of two or three, and be the first to 25.

Play your way:
- Play a friend online. Create a game, send the link or show the QR code, and play from anywhere.
- Play the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard.
- Two players on one device, face to face.
- The daily challenge: one position, one try. Keep your streak alive.

Made for everyone:
- Easy to learn, with the rules built in.
- No account, no advertising, no in-app purchases.
- No chat. Online play uses five preset reactions only.
- Works without a connection for every mode except online play.
- Wooden board, seed sounds and a pixel-art title screen.

Oware King is made in South Africa by Object91.

## Age and content
- Content rating: the lowest band on every store (no violence, no gambling). The only user-written text shown to others is a short player name.
- Google Play "Target audience": tick 13-15, 16-17 and 18+ only. Do not tick any group under 13, or the Families policy applies. Answer "No" to "Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children?".
- App Store: age rating 4+, but do not choose the Kids category and do not use "for kids" or "for children" wording anywhere in the listing.
- Players can interact online: yes, with a name and five preset reactions only.
- Data collected: only for online play. A player name and a random device ID are sent to the server with the moves and are deleted one day after the game was last used. Nothing is collected in the other modes. No data is sold or shared, and there is no tracking or analytics.

### Google Play "Data safety" answers
- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? Yes.
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Yes.
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Yes (it is deleted automatically after one day; requests by email to the support address).
- Data types: "Personal info > Name" (the player name) and "Device or other IDs" (the random ID).
- For both: Collected: yes. Shared: no. Processed ephemerally: no. Required or optional: optional (only when the player chooses online play). Purpose: App functionality.

### Apple "App Privacy" answers
- Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app? Yes.
- Data types: "Contact Info > Name" (the player name), "Identifiers > Device ID" (the random ID), "User Content > Gameplay Content" (moves and reactions).
- For each: used for App Functionality only. Linked to the user's identity: no. Used for tracking: no.

### LG Seller Lounge and Microsoft Store
