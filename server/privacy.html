Oware King privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. Oware King is published by Object91, South Africa.

Summary

Oware King has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. You can play the computer, two players on one device, and the daily challenge without sending us anything.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: sound on or off, and the computer level you chose.
  • Your results: your daily challenge streak and how many times you beat the computer.
  • The name you typed for online play, and a random ID that lets you return to a game you were playing.

This is kept in your browser or app storage. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site or app data.

What is sent when you play a friend online

  • The name you typed. Your friend sees it. Please do not use your full real name.
  • The random ID from your device, so the game knows which seat is yours. It does not identify you as a person.
  • Your moves, rematch requests and reactions (five preset pictures). There is no chat.

A game and everything in it is deleted from our server one day after it was last used.

Our hosting provider

The game is served by Cloudflare. Like every website host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the game and to protect it from abuse. We do not use this to identify players. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

Oware King is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing a player types is a name for online play, and it is deleted with the game after one day.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws elsewhere, you may ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because games are deleted after one day and we keep no accounts, we normally hold nothing.

Changes
